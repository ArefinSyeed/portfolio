Our Farm Story

Rooted in the countryside, built on trust.

Farmora began with a simple idea: dates should taste the way they do in the grove, not the way they do after months in a warehouse. We work with growers, harvest in season, and keep each variety separate so you always know what you are eating.

  1. 1

    Picked at the right time

    Bunches are harvested when each variety is ready, not before.

  2. 2

    Sorted by hand

    Size, softness and color are checked by people, not left to chance.

  3. 3

    Packed fresh

    Sealed in food-safe packs to lock in moisture and flavor.

  4. 4

    Delivered to your door

    From our packing table to your home, anywhere in Bangladesh.

Good to Know

How should I store dates?⌄

Keep them in a cool, dry place in an airtight container. For longer storage, the fridge keeps soft dates fresh for months.

Which date is best for beginners?⌄

Try Medjool for a soft, caramel-like taste, or Sukkari if you want something very sweet. The Tasting Box lets you try several.
